# Engineering Standard

* [Hazard and Operability Analysis (HAZOP) Methodology Standard](hazop-methodology.md) - Standard technical specification, principles, governing standards (IEC 61882, ICH Q9), scoping perspectives, advantages, and limitations of Hazard and Operability (HAZOP) studies.
* [Risk Assessment Matrix (RAM) and Risk Assessment Code (RAC) Standard](ram-methodology.md) - Qualitative risk evaluation standard consolidating severity, probability, and exposure into 4-tier Risk Assessment Codes (RAC 1 to 4) with mandatory mitigation action protocols.
