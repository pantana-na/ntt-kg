# S-11 — Service Water Strainer S-11 (Train A)

## Function

Nuclear safety-related automatic in-line basket strainer for Service Water Train A, located in the Primary Auxiliary Building (PAB), removing marine debris, silt, and macro-fouling from ocean seawater before it enters the primary component cooling water heat exchangers and diesel generator jacket coolers.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Debris blinding or biofouling causing excessive differential pressure across strainer basket monitored by PDIS 8259.
- Unfiltered raw seawater entering nuclear safety heat exchangers if bypass valve V67 is inadvertently opened during operation.
