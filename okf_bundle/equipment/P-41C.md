## Hazards & Safeguards

- Loss of suction head due to intake forebay low water level monitored by LT-8260.
- Pump discharge overpressurization or water hammer mitigated by discharge check valve 1-SW-V21 and expansion joint EP-44.
- Biofouling and silt accumulation from Atlantic Ocean seawater requiring continuous minimum flow through restriction orifice RO-6106.
