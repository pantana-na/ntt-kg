# SS-E-106 — Post Accident Sample System Heat Exchanger

## Function

Post Accident Sample System (PASS) heat exchanger designed to rapidly cool highly radioactive post-accident reactor coolant fluid drawn from RCS Loops 1 & 3 hot legs down to manageable temperatures prior to grab sampling in shielded sample casks TK-228A and TK-228B. Cooled by CCW Loop A.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Severe radiation hazard: Ingests post-LOCA primary coolant with elevated fission product concentrations. Located inside shielded PASS panel cubicle.
- Thermal burn and sample flashing hazard: Fluid must be cooled below saturation before entering sample casks; monitored by in-line thermowell TI-2996.
