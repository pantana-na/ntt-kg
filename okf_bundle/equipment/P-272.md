# P-272 — Refueling Canal Skimmer Pump

## Function

Refueling Canal Skimmer Pump taking suction from refueling pool surface skimmers S-245A..E and bottom floor drains (via Reactor Cavity Cleanup Filter F-207), circulating water for clarity and purification during refueling outages, and transferring cavity water through Containment Penetration X-39 to CVCS demineralizers, floor drain tanks, or the fuel pool prefilter.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Containment isolation: Discharge penetrates Containment at penetration X-39 with inside containment isolation valve SF-V86 and outside locked-closed isolation valve SF-V87. SF-V87 must be locked closed during power operation.
- Thermal expansion / overpressurization: Thermal relief valve SF-V101 set at 150 PSIG protects the isolated containment penetration piping run between SF-V86 and SF-V87.
- Mechanical seal integrity: Dedicated seal water supply required per Note 6 (Drawing 20353).
