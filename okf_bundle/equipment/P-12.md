# P-12 — Spent Fuel Pool Skimmer Pump

## Function

Spent Fuel Pool Skimmer Pump taking suction from surface skimmers S-2A through S-2E and pool headers, circulating surface pool water through Prefilter F-33, Demineralizer DM-8, and Postfilter F-34 for particulate and ionic purification before discharging back to the Spent Fuel Pool.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Overpressurization protection provided by 1-1/2" relief valve 1-SF-V204 set at 150 PSIG discharging to floor drain.
