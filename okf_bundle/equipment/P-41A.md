# P-41A — Service Water Pump 41A (Ocean Intake Train A)

## Function

Primary nuclear safety-related ocean intake service water pump for Train A, taking suction from the Service Water Pumphouse forebay well and discharging into the 24" Train A nuclear service water header to provide continuous cooling water to primary component cooling water heat exchanger CC-E-17A, diesel generator jacket cooler DG-E-42B, and secondary cooling loads.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Loss of suction head due to intake forebay low water level monitored by LT-8260.
- Pump discharge overpressurization or water hammer mitigated by discharge check valve 1-SW-V1 and expansion joint EP-42.
- Biofouling and silt accumulation from Atlantic Ocean seawater requiring continuous minimum flow through restriction orifice RO-6104 and periodic inspection.
