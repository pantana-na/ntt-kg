# F-33 — Fuel Pool Prefilter

## Function

Fuel Pool Prefilter removing particulates and suspended solids from spent fuel pool water and cavity cleanup flows prior to entry into ion-exchange Demineralizer DM-8, protecting downstream resin beds from fouling.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- High differential pressure / element fouling: Monitored across filter shell by PDIS-2622. Operators must switch to bypass V25 or isolate when DP setpoint is reached.
- Filter venting gas accumulation: Top vent V55 routes to manifold through common sight glass FG-2612 per General Note 4 to confirm liquid filling before startup.
- Radioactive waste handling: Bottom drain V41 discharges potentially contaminated water to CS DRN HDR / WLD OR 183.
