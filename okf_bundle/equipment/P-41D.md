# P-41D — Service Water Pump 41D (Ocean Intake Train B)

## Function

Primary nuclear safety-related ocean intake service water pump for Train B (parallel with P-41B), taking suction from the Service Water Pumphouse forebay well and discharging into the 24" Train B nuclear service water header.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Loss of suction head due to intake forebay low water level monitored by LT-8260.
- Pump discharge overpressurization or water hammer mitigated by discharge check valve 1-SW-V30 and expansion joint EP-45.
- Biofouling and silt accumulation from Atlantic Ocean seawater requiring continuous minimum flow through restriction orifice RO-6108.
