# P-41B — Service Water Pump 41B (Ocean Intake Train B)

## Function

Primary nuclear safety-related ocean intake service water pump for Train B, taking suction from the Service Water Pumphouse forebay well and discharging into the 24" Train B nuclear service water header to provide continuous cooling water to primary component cooling water heat exchanger CC-E-17B, diesel generator jacket cooler DG-E-42A, and secondary cooling loads.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Loss of suction head due to intake forebay low water level monitored by LT-8260.
- Pump discharge overpressurization or water hammer mitigated by discharge check valve 1-SW-V28 and expansion joint EP-43.
- Biofouling and silt accumulation from Atlantic Ocean seawater requiring continuous minimum flow through restriction orifice RO-6107 and periodic inspection.
