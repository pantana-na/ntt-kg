# SS-T-312 — Post Accident Liquid Drainer

## Function

Post Accident Liquid Drainer T-312 acting as an in-line moisture and liquid knockout separator on the Flush Tank TK-197 off-gas vent line, preventing entrained radioactive moisture carryover into the PAB ventilation ducting while discharging condensate to liquid waste drainage.
