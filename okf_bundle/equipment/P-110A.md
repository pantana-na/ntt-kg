# P-110A — Cooling Tower Service Water Pump 110A (Train A)

## Function

Safety-related Ultimate Heat Sink (UHS) cooling tower service water pump for Train A, taking suction from the Service Water Cooling Tower cold water basin and discharging into the Train A nuclear service water distribution system during emergency ocean tunnel isolation, design basis events, or ultimate heat sink alignment.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Loss of ultimate heat sink inventory or vortexing if basin level drops below minimum design level.
- Pump discharge check valve slamming or water hammer mitigated by check valve V53 and expansion joint EP-40.
- Low temperature freeze hazard during winter operations mitigated by basin temperature monitoring TE-6152A/B/C.
