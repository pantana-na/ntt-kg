## Function

## Hazards & Safeguards

- High differential pressure across cartridge elements indicates crud loading; monitored locally via PDI-2682.
- Valves SF-V191 and SF-V192 are locked/sealed open (`G`) during operation to prevent inadvertent isolation and deadheading during cavity purification.
