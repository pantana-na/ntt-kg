## Hazards & Safeguards

- Contaminated gas discharge: Vacuum pump exhaust filtered by F-193 and vented within the ventilated enclosure of sample sink MM-13B.
- Suction line isolation: 1/4" vacuum valves SS-V832, SS-V833, SS-V834 provide positive isolation of vacuum headers.
