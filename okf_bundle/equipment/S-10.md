# S-10 — Service Water Strainer S-10 (Train B)

## Function

Nuclear safety-related automatic in-line basket strainer for Service Water Train B, located in the Primary Auxiliary Building (PAB), removing marine debris, silt, and macro-fouling from ocean seawater before it enters CC-E-17B, DG-E-42A, and associated cooling circuits.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Debris blinding or biofouling causing excessive differential pressure across strainer basket monitored by PDIS 8258.
- Unfiltered raw seawater entering nuclear safety heat exchangers if bypass valve V70 is inadvertently opened during operation.
