# Equipment Concept

* [1-SS-E-14A — Steam Generator 1 Primary Sample Heat Exchanger](1-SS-E-14A.md) - Primary sample cooler for Steam Generator 1 blowdown sample stream, cooling high-temperature, high-pressure blowdown water against Component Cooling Water Loop A prior to secondary constant temperature cooling in E-173 and chemical/radiological analysis.
* [1-SS-E-14B — Steam Generator 2 Primary Sample Heat Exchanger](1-SS-E-14B.md) - Primary sample cooler for Steam Generator 2 blowdown sample stream, cooling high-temperature blowdown water against CCW Loop A prior to secondary constant temperature cooling in E-174 and chemical/radiological analysis.
* [1-SS-E-14C — Steam Generator 3 Primary Sample Heat Exchanger](1-SS-E-14C.md) - Primary sample cooler for Steam Generator 3 blowdown sample stream, cooling high-temperature blowdown water against CCW Loop A prior to secondary constant temperature cooling in E-175 and chemical/radiological analysis.
* [1-SS-E-14D — Steam Generator 4 Primary Sample Heat Exchanger](1-SS-E-14D.md) - Primary sample cooler for Steam Generator 4 blowdown sample stream, cooling high-temperature blowdown water against CCW Loop A prior to secondary constant temperature cooling in E-176 and chemical/radiological analysis.
* [CBS-EP-36A — Expansion Bellows / Piece A](CBS-EP-36A.md) - Safety Class 2 16" flexible expansion bellows installed on the Containment Recirculation Sump A suction line inside Protective Chamber CBS-TK-101A downstream of penetration X-61, providing seismic and thermal expansion accommodation while maintaining post-LOCA containment and ECCS suction boundary integrity.
* [CBS-EP-36B — Expansion Bellows / Piece B](CBS-EP-36B.md) - Safety Class 2 16" flexible expansion bellows installed on the Containment Recirculation Sump B suction line inside Protective Chamber CBS-TK-101B downstream of penetration X-60, providing seismic and thermal expansion accommodation while maintaining post-LOCA containment and ECCS suction boundary integrity.
* [CBS-TK-101A — Protective Chamber A (Sump A Guard Pipe Enclosure)](CBS-TK-101A.md) - Safety Class 2 sealed protective chamber enclosing the containment recirculation sump A suction line, expansion bellows CBS-EP-36A, and motor-operated sump isolation valve CBS-V8, providing containment isolation barrier redundancy and leak detection via level switch LS-2356 outside containment penetration X-61.
* [CBS-TK-101B — Protective Chamber B (Sump B Guard Pipe Enclosure)](CBS-TK-101B.md) - Safety Class 2 sealed protective chamber enclosing the containment recirculation sump B suction line, expansion bellows CBS-EP-36B, and motor-operated sump isolation valve CBS-V14, providing containment isolation barrier redundancy and leak detection via level switch LS-2357 outside containment penetration X-60.
* [CBS-TK-10A — Containment Recirculation Sump A](CBS-TK-10A.md) - Containment Recirculation Sump A providing a qualified suction reservoir for emergency core cooling and containment spray systems during post-LOCA recirculation mode. Features concentric guard pipe 1-CBS-S-611-A, expansion joint CBS-EP-36A, sealed protective chamber CBS-TK-101A housing motor-operated isolation valve CBS-V8, and level instrumentation.
* [CBS-TK-10B — Containment Recirculation Sump B](CBS-TK-10B.md) - Containment Recirculation Sump B providing a redundant, qualified suction reservoir for emergency core cooling and containment spray systems during post-LOCA recirculation mode. Features concentric guard pipe 1-CBS-S-611-B, expansion joint CBS-EP-36B, sealed protective chamber CBS-TK-101B housing motor-operated isolation valve CBS-V14, and level instrumentation.
* [CC-E-17A — Primary Component Cooling Water Heat Exchanger A](CC-E-17A.md) - Primary nuclear safety-related component cooling water heat exchanger for Train A, transferring heat from the closed-loop Primary Component Cooling Water System (shell side) to the open-loop Nuclear Service Water System (tube side, titanium tubes), providing ultimate cooling to reactor coolant pump thermal barriers, residual heat removal heat exchangers, and safety injection systems.
* [CC-E-17B — Primary Component Cooling Water Heat Exchanger B](CC-E-17B.md) - Primary nuclear safety-related component cooling water heat exchanger for Train B, transferring heat from the closed-loop Primary Component Cooling Water System (shell side) to the open-loop Nuclear Service Water System (tube side, titanium tubes).
* [CS-P-243A — Charging Pump CS-P-2A Lube Oil Pump](CS-P-243A.md) - Auxiliary positive displacement lubrication oil pump for Charging / High Head Safety Injection Pump CS-P-2A, circulating lube oil from oil reservoir through oil cooler and bearings to maintain hydrodynamic bearing lubrication during normal and emergency injection service.
* [CS-P-243B — Charging Pump CS-P-2B Lube Oil Pump](CS-P-243B.md) - Auxiliary positive displacement lubrication oil pump for Charging / High Head Safety Injection Pump CS-P-2B, circulating lube oil from oil reservoir through oil cooler and bearings to maintain hydrodynamic bearing lubrication during normal and emergency injection service.
* [CS-P-2A — Charging Pump (High Head Safety Injection)](CS-P-2A.md) - High Head Safety Injection / Charging Pump A supplying normal CVCS reactor coolant makeup, pressurizer level control, reactor coolant pump (RCP) seal water injection, and delivering high-pressure emergency borated water from the RWST to RCS Cold Legs (Loops 1–4) through Penetration X-24 during emergency safety injection (LOCA / Steam Line Break).
* [CS-P-2B — Charging Pump (High Head Safety Injection)](CS-P-2B.md) - High Head Safety Injection / Charging Pump B supplying redundant normal CVCS reactor coolant makeup, pressurizer level control, reactor coolant pump (RCP) seal water injection, and delivering high-pressure emergency borated water from the RWST to RCS Cold Legs (Loops 1–4) through Penetration X-24 during emergency safety injection (LOCA / Steam Line Break).
* [DG-E-42A — Diesel Generator 1A Water Jacket Heat Exchanger](DG-E-42A.md) - Nuclear safety-related water jacket heat exchanger for Emergency Diesel Generator 1A (Train A), cooled by Service Water Train B, providing engine cooling during emergency loss of offsite power or safety injection actuation. Controlled by fail-open valve FO 8291 upon DG-1A start command.
* [DG-E-42B — Diesel Generator 1B Water Jacket Heat Exchanger](DG-E-42B.md) - Nuclear safety-related water jacket heat exchanger for Emergency Diesel Generator 1B (Train B), cooled by Service Water Train A, providing engine cooling during emergency loss of offsite power or safety injection actuation. Controlled by fail-open valve FO 8292 upon DG-1B start command.
* [DM-8 — Fuel Pool Demineralizer](DM-8.md) - Fuel Pool Demineralizer providing deep-bed ion-exchange purification of spent fuel pool water and cavity cleanup streams, removing soluble radionuclides, fission products, and ionic impurities to maintain pool water clarity and minimize radiation fields.
* [F-207 — Reactor Cavity Cleanup Filter](F-207.md) - Reactor Cavity Cleanup Filter providing particulate and crud filtration for the Refueling Pool & Canal water inside primary containment during refueling outages. Equipped with locked/sealed isolation valves SF-V191/SF-V192, differential pressure indicator PDI-2682, top vent SF-V105, and bottom drain SF-V106.
* [F-33 — Fuel Pool Prefilter](F-33.md) - Fuel Pool Prefilter removing particulates and suspended solids from spent fuel pool water and cavity cleanup flows prior to entry into ion-exchange Demineralizer DM-8, protecting downstream resin beds from fouling.
* [F-34 — Fuel Pool Postfilter](F-34.md) - Fuel Pool Postfilter installed downstream of Demineralizer DM-8 to capture resin fines, particulates, and resin fragments, preventing radioactive resin carryover into the Spent Fuel Pool. Effluent discharges through a 4" return header terminating 11 ft above spent fuel assemblies.
* [FN-40A — Service Water Pumphouse Supply Fan 40A](FN-40A.md) - Nuclear safety-related ventilation supply fan for the Service Water Pumphouse (Train A), delivering 3,400 CFM of filtered outside air to the Train A and Train B electrical switchgear rooms and pump house floor to maintain equipment ambient temperatures within qualified design limits during normal and post-accident operations.
* [FN-40B — Service Water Pumphouse Supply Fan 40B](FN-40B.md) - Nuclear safety-related ventilation supply fan for the Service Water Pumphouse (Train B), delivering 3,400 CFM of filtered outside air to the Train A and Train B electrical switchgear rooms and pump house floor to maintain equipment ambient temperatures within qualified design limits during normal and post-accident operations.
* [FN-51A — Service Water Cooling Tower Fan 51A](FN-51A.md) - Induced-draft nuclear safety-related cooling tower fan for Service Water Cooling Tower Cell A, drawing air through the spray nozzle fill pack to reject decay heat from the Nuclear Service Water System to the atmosphere during Ultimate Heat Sink operation.
* [FN-51B — Service Water Cooling Tower Fan 51B](FN-51B.md) - Induced-draft nuclear safety-related cooling tower fan for Service Water Cooling Tower Cell B, drawing air through the spray nozzle fill pack to reject decay heat from the Nuclear Service Water System to the atmosphere during Ultimate Heat Sink operation.
* [P-110A — Cooling Tower Service Water Pump 110A (Train A)](P-110A.md) - Safety-related Ultimate Heat Sink (UHS) cooling tower service water pump for Train A, taking suction from the Service Water Cooling Tower cold water basin and discharging into the Train A nuclear service water distribution system during emergency ocean tunnel isolation, design basis events, or ultimate heat sink alignment.
* [P-110B — Cooling Tower Service Water Pump 110B (Train B)](P-110B.md) - Safety-related Ultimate Heat Sink (UHS) cooling tower service water pump for Train B, taking suction from the Service Water Cooling Tower cold water basin and discharging into the Train B nuclear service water distribution system during emergency ocean tunnel isolation, design basis events, or ultimate heat sink alignment.
* [P-12 — Spent Fuel Pool Skimmer Pump](P-12.md) - Spent Fuel Pool Skimmer Pump taking suction from surface skimmers S-2A through S-2E and pool headers, circulating surface pool water through Prefilter F-33, Demineralizer DM-8, and Postfilter F-34 for particulate and ionic purification before discharging back to the Spent Fuel Pool.
* [P-272 — Refueling Canal Skimmer Pump](P-272.md) - Refueling Canal Skimmer Pump taking suction from refueling pool surface skimmers S-245A..E and bottom floor drains (via Reactor Cavity Cleanup Filter F-207), circulating water for clarity and purification during refueling outages, and transferring cavity water through Containment Penetration X-39 to CVCS demineralizers, floor drain tanks, or the fuel pool prefilter.
* [P-41A — Service Water Pump 41A (Ocean Intake Train A)](P-41A.md) - Primary nuclear safety-related ocean intake service water pump for Train A, taking suction from the Service Water Pumphouse forebay well and discharging into the 24" Train A nuclear service water header to provide continuous cooling water to primary component cooling water heat exchanger CC-E-17A, diesel generator jacket cooler DG-E-42B, and secondary cooling loads.
* [P-41B — Service Water Pump 41B (Ocean Intake Train B)](P-41B.md) - Primary nuclear safety-related ocean intake service water pump for Train B, taking suction from the Service Water Pumphouse forebay well and discharging into the 24" Train B nuclear service water header to provide continuous cooling water to primary component cooling water heat exchanger CC-E-17B, diesel generator jacket cooler DG-E-42A, and secondary cooling loads.
* [P-41C — Service Water Pump 41C (Ocean Intake Train A)](P-41C.md) - Primary nuclear safety-related ocean intake service water pump for Train A (parallel with P-41A), taking suction from the Service Water Pumphouse forebay well and discharging into the 24" Train A nuclear service water header.
* [P-41D — Service Water Pump 41D (Ocean Intake Train B)](P-41D.md) - Primary nuclear safety-related ocean intake service water pump for Train B (parallel with P-41B), taking suction from the Service Water Pumphouse forebay well and discharging into the 24" Train B nuclear service water header.
* [RH-E-9A — RHR Loop A Heat Exchanger](RH-E-9A.md) - Residual Heat Removal Loop A Heat Exchanger cooling circulating reactor coolant during normal plant shutdown / cooldown and cooling emergency post-LOCA recirculation water from Containment Recirculation Sump A for low-head safety injection into the RCS Cold Legs and SI Accumulators. Cooled by 20" Component Cooling Water Loop A. Residual Heat Removal Loop A Heat Exchanger cooling circulating reactor coolant during normal plant shutdown/cooldown and cooling emergency post-LOCA recirculation water from Containment Recirculation Sump A for low-head safety injection into RCS Loops 1 and 2 Cold Legs and SI Accumulators via Penetration X-11. Also provides suction supply to High Head SI Pump SI-P-6A via valve RH-V22.
* [RH-E-9B — RHR Loop B Heat Exchanger](RH-E-9B.md) - Residual Heat Removal Loop B Heat Exchanger cooling circulating reactor coolant during normal plant shutdown / cooldown and cooling emergency post-LOCA recirculation water from Containment Recirculation Sump B for low-head safety injection into the RCS Cold Legs and SI Accumulators. Cooled by 20" Component Cooling Water Loop B. Residual Heat Removal Loop B Heat Exchanger cooling circulating reactor coolant during normal plant shutdown/cooldown and cooling emergency post-LOCA recirculation water from Containment Recirculation Sump B for low-head safety injection into RCS Loops 3 and 4 Cold Legs and SI Accumulators via Penetration X-12. Also provides suction supply to Intermediate/High Head SI Pump SI-P-6B via valve RH-V21.
* [RH-P-8A — Residual Heat Removal Pump A](RH-P-8A.md) - Residual Heat Removal (RHR) Pump A providing dual-function service: normal shutdown decay heat removal by drawing reactor coolant from RCS Loop 1 Hot Leg through Penetration X-9 and circulating through RHR Heat Exchanger RH-E-9A, and emergency Low Head Safety Injection (LHSI) / post-LOCA sump recirculation by drawing from Containment Recirculation Sump A (CBS-TK-10A) and delivering cooled water to RCS Cold Legs and SI Accumulators. Residual Heat Removal (RHR) Pump A providing dual-function service: normal shutdown decay heat removal by drawing reactor coolant from RCS Loop 1 Hot Leg through Penetration X-9 and circulating through RHR Heat Exchanger RH-E-9A, and emergency Low Head Safety Injection (LHSI) / post-LOCA sump recirculation by drawing from Containment Recirculation Sump A (CBS-TK-10A) and delivering cooled water to RCS Cold Legs and SI Accumulators via MOV RH-V18 and Penetration X-11.
* [RH-P-8B — Residual Heat Removal Pump B](RH-P-8B.md) - Residual Heat Removal (RHR) Pump B providing redundant shutdown decay heat removal by drawing reactor coolant from RCS Loop 4 Hot Leg through Penetration X-10 and circulating through RHR Heat Exchanger RH-E-9B, and emergency Low Head Safety Injection (LHSI) / post-LOCA sump recirculation by drawing from Containment Recirculation Sump B (CBS-TK-10B) and delivering cooled water to RCS Cold Legs and SI Accumulators. Residual Heat Removal (RHR) Pump B providing dual-function service: normal shutdown decay heat removal by drawing reactor coolant from RCS Loop 4 Hot Leg through Penetration X-10 and circulating through RHR Heat Exchanger RH-E-9B, and emergency Low Head Safety Injection (LHSI) / post-LOCA sump recirculation by drawing from Containment Recirculation Sump B (CBS-TK-10B) and delivering cooled water to RCS Cold Legs and SI Accumulators via MOV RH-V19 and Penetration X-12.
* [S-10 — Service Water Strainer S-10 (Train B)](S-10.md) - Nuclear safety-related automatic in-line basket strainer for Service Water Train B, located in the Primary Auxiliary Building (PAB), removing marine debris, silt, and macro-fouling from ocean seawater before it enters CC-E-17B, DG-E-42A, and associated cooling circuits.
* [S-11 — Service Water Strainer S-11 (Train A)](S-11.md) - Nuclear safety-related automatic in-line basket strainer for Service Water Train A, located in the Primary Auxiliary Building (PAB), removing marine debris, silt, and macro-fouling from ocean seawater before it enters the primary component cooling water heat exchangers and diesel generator jacket coolers.
* [SCC-E-29A — Secondary Component Cooling Water Heat Exchanger A](SCC-E-29A.md) - Secondary component cooling water heat exchanger for Train A, located in the Turbine Building, cooled by Nuclear Service Water to remove heat from conventional turbine-generator auxiliary cooling loops.
* [SCC-E-29B — Secondary Component Cooling Water Heat Exchanger B](SCC-E-29B.md) - Secondary component cooling water heat exchanger for Train B, located in the Turbine Building, cooled by Nuclear Service Water to remove heat from conventional turbine-generator auxiliary cooling loops.
* [SF-E-15C — Spent Fuel Pool Heat Exchanger C (Safety Class 3, Abandoned / Isolated)](SF-E-15C.md) - Third Spent Fuel Pool cooling heat exchanger (Safety Class 3) originally installed for Alternate Spent Fuel Pool Cooling (ASFPC). Under License Renewal scope, SF-E-15C is isolated and drained, with SW service water supply/return lines permanently blind flanged and disconnected under the Equipment to be Abandoned Program.
* [SI-P-6A — Safety Injection Pump A (Intermediate Head)](SI-P-6A.md) - Intermediate Head Safety Injection Pump A providing high-pressure emergency core cooling water from the Refueling Water Storage Tank (RWST) or Cold Leg/Hot Leg recirculation headers to the Reactor Coolant System (RCS) Loops during loss-of-coolant accidents (LOCA). Intermediate Head Safety Injection Pump A providing high-pressure emergency core cooling water from RWST or post-LOCA low-head recirculation cross-tie (via MOV RH-V22) delivering to RCS Loops 1 and 2 cold legs.
* [SI-P-6B — Safety Injection Pump B (Intermediate Head)](SI-P-6B.md) - Intermediate Head Safety Injection Pump B providing redundant high-pressure emergency core cooling water from the Refueling Water Storage Tank (RWST) or Cold Leg/Hot Leg recirculation headers to the Reactor Coolant System (RCS) Loops during loss-of-coolant accidents (LOCA). Intermediate Head Safety Injection Pump B providing high-pressure emergency core cooling water from RWST or post-LOCA low-head recirculation cross-tie (via MOV RH-V21) delivering to RCS Loops 3 and 4 cold legs.

* [SS-CP-166A — Primary Sample Panel CP-166A & Sink MM-13B](SS-CP-166A.md) - Nuclear Primary Sample Panel CP-166A and Sink MM-13B enclosure housing sample pressure reduction, analytical instrumentation, dissolved gas analysis (O2 and H2), grab sample bomb and septum stations, and radioactive drainage collection for RCS, CVCS, and RHR process samples.
* [SS-CP-419 — Post Accident Sample Panel CP-419](SS-CP-419.md) - Post Accident Sample Panel CP-419 enclosure structure located within the shielded PASS panel cubicle in the Primary Auxiliary Building. * [SS-E-106 — Post Accident Sample System Heat Exchanger](SS-E-106.md) - Post Accident Sample System (PASS) heat exchanger designed to rapidly cool highly radioactive post-accident reactor coolant fluid drawn from RCS Loops 1 & 3 hot legs down to manageable temperatures prior to grab sampling in shielded sample casks TK-228A and TK-228B. Cooled by CCW Loop A.
* [SS-E-12A — Pressurizer Liquid & Steam Sample Heat Exchanger A](SS-E-12A.md) - Primary Nuclear Sample System heat exchanger cooling high-temperature, high-pressure borated reactor coolant from the Pressurizer steam or liquid space prior to grab sampling at Primary Sample Panel CP-166A. Arranged in series with cooler SS-E-12B and cooled by Component Cooling Water Loop A.
* [SS-E-12B — Pressurizer Liquid & Steam Sample Heat Exchanger B](SS-E-12B.md) - Second-stage nuclear sample heat exchanger arranged in series with SS-E-12A to cool pressurizer steam and liquid sample streams down to safe grab-sampling temperatures prior to discharge to Primary Sample Panel CP-166A. Cooled by CCW Loop A.
* [SS-E-13A — RC Loop 1 & 3 Liquid Sample Heat Exchanger A](SS-E-13A.md) - Primary Nuclear Sample System heat exchanger cooling high-pressure, high-temperature borated liquid drawn from Reactor Coolant System Loop 1 or Loop 3 hot legs. Upstream flow throttled to 0.8 gpm via reach-rod valve RC-V345. Arranged in series with cooler SS-E-13B and cooled by Component Cooling Water Loop A.
* [SS-E-13B — RC Loop 1 & 3 Liquid Sample Heat Exchanger B](SS-E-13B.md) - Second-stage nuclear sample heat exchanger arranged in series with SS-E-13A to cool reactor coolant liquid from loops 1 and 3 down to safe grab-sampling temperatures prior to discharge to Primary Sample Panel CP-166A. Cooled by CCW Loop A.
* [SS-P-392 — Primary Sample Sink Vacuum Pump](SS-P-392.md) - Auxiliary vacuum pump providing vacuum evacuation for sample containers, flexible hoses, and Primary Sample Sink MM-13B analytical apparatus. Equipped with suction particulate filter SS-F-192 and discharge aerosol filter SS-F-193.
* [SS-T-312 — Post Accident Liquid Drainer](SS-T-312.md) - Post Accident Liquid Drainer T-312 acting as an in-line moisture and liquid knockout separator on the Flush Tank TK-197 off-gas vent line, preventing entrained radioactive moisture carryover into the PAB ventilation ducting while discharging condensate to liquid waste drainage.
* [SS-TK-197 — Post Accident Flush Tank](SS-TK-197.md) - Post Accident Sample System Flush Tank accumulating radioactive demineralized flush water and residual coolant drainage from the PASS sample casks and tubing before transferring effluent inside containment through Penetration X-19 to Waste Process Liquid Drains.
* [SS-TK-228A — Post Accident Sample Cask A](SS-TK-228A.md) - Heavily shielded Post Accident Sample Cask designed to safely obtain, contain, and transport highly radioactive grab samples of post-LOCA reactor coolant liquid for radiochemical and isotopic laboratory analysis under 10 CFR 50.34(f)(2)(viii) PASS criteria.
* [SS-TK-228B — Post Accident Sample Cask B](SS-TK-228B.md) - Redundant heavily shielded Post Accident Sample Cask designed to obtain, contain, and transport highly radioactive grab samples of post-LOCA reactor coolant liquid for radiochemical and isotopic laboratory analysis under 10 CFR 50.34(f)(2)(viii) PASS criteria.
