# PT-614 — RHR Loop A Suction Header Pressure Transmitter

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Residual Heat Removal Pump suction pressure monitoring is critical during shutdown cooling operations to prevent pump cavitation and vortexing during mid-loop and low reactor coolant level operations. Inadequate suction pressure threatens RHR pump mechanical seals and core decay heat removal capability.

## Operating Philosophy & Functional Logic

* **Process Function:** Measures static suction pressure in the 8" RHR Loop A suction line drawing from RCS Loop 1 Hot Leg (via penetration X-9) or Containment Sump A.
* **Control Board Interface:** Transmits a continuous electronic pressure signal to Main Control Board indicators and alarms.
* **NPSH & Low-Pressure Alarms:** Provides early detection of suction line voiding, degassing, or inadequate net positive suction head during plant cooldown and refueling outages.
