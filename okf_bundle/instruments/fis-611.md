# FIS-611 — RHR Loop B Suction Flow Indicating Switch & Bypass Interlock

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> FIS-611 monitors suction flow from the RCS Loop 4 Hot Leg to RHR Pump RH-P-8B and interlocks with Heat Exchanger bypass control valve RH-FCV-611 (Interlock H per Note 6). Valve misalignment or improper flow balancing can starve minimum flow to RH-P-8B or induce water hammer during transition from cold leg injection to recirculation modes.

## 2. Process & Functional Architecture

- **RHR Flow Regulation:** Suction flow from RCS Loop 4 Hot Leg passes through containment penetration X-10, isolation valve CBS-V25, and FE-611 into pump RH-P-8B. FIS-611 measures suction flow and coordinates bypass valve modulation.
- **Bypass Control & Protection:** FIS-611 interlocks with valve RH-FCV-611 (Interlock H) to govern bypass flow around heat exchanger RH-E-9B, ensuring consistent hydraulic throughput and preventing pump cavitation under varying primary system conditions.
