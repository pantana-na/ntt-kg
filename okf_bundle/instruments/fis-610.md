# FIS-610 — RHR Loop A Suction Flow Indicating Switch & Bypass Interlock

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> FIS-610 monitors suction flow from the RCS Loop 1 Hot Leg to RHR Pump RH-P-8A and interlocks with Heat Exchanger bypass control valve RH-FCV-610 (Interlock G per Note 6). Misalignment or premature closure of RH-FCV-610 under low suction flow risks pump runout or cavitation during reactor cooldown operations.

## 2. Process & Functional Architecture

- **RHR Flow Regulation:** During normal plant shutdown and decay heat removal, suction flow from RCS Loop 1 Hot Leg passes through containment penetration X-9, isolation valve CBS-V26, and FE-610 to pump RH-P-8A. FIS-610 indicates local flow and provides interlock logic to valve RH-FCV-610.
- **Cooldown Rate & Bypass Management:** By modulating bypass valve RH-FCV-610 in conjunction with heat exchanger inlet flow, operators control total RHR loop flow and cooldown rate while ensuring total loop flow remains within pump hydraulic operating envelopes.
