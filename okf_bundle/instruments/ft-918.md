# FT-918 — Intermediate Head Safety Injection Pump A Discharge Flow Transmitter

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Accurate flow measurement on the discharge of Safety Injection Pump SI-P-6A is essential for confirming intermediate head safety injection capability during small-to-intermediate break loss-of-coolant accidents (LOCA). Inadequate flow delivery signals pump degradation, valve mispositioning, or system runout, requiring immediate operator action to adjust discharge runout throttle valve SI-V96 per Note 7.

## Operating Philosophy & Functional Logic

* **Process Function:** Senses differential pressure across primary flow element FE-918 in the 4" discharge line of Safety Injection Pump SI-P-6A and transmits an electronic flow signal to Main Control Board instrumentation.
* **Accident Mitigation Role:** Provides operators and emergency operating procedures (EOPs) with direct verification of borated water delivery into the reactor coolant system hot legs and cold legs via penetration X-25.
* **Runout & Flow Balancing:** Used in conjunction with manual throttle valve SI-V96 and downstream cold leg / hot leg restricting orifices (RO-2691, RO-2692) to verify proper flow distribution and prevent pump motor overload during runout conditions.
