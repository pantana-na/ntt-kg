# LT-2629 — Refueling Cavity Water Level Transmitter

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Refueling cavity water level is a primary nuclear safety critical parameter during reactor refueling and fuel transfer operations. Sufficient water inventory (> 23 ft above irradiated fuel assemblies) is strictly required by plant technical specifications to provide biological radiation shielding and decay heat sink. Inadvertent draining of the cavity through skimmer lines, bottom drains, or filter manifolds creates an immediate radiological hazard.

## Operating Philosophy & Functional Logic

* **Process Function:** Continuous electronic measurement of the water level in the reactor refueling pool and transfer canal during refueling outages and flooded conditions.
* **Control Room Interface:** Transmits real-time cavity level signal to Main Control Room instrumentation, supporting technical specification compliance verification during fuel movement.
* **Low / High Level Alarms:** Provides low-level alarms to warn operators of inventory loss through cavity drain valves (SF-V80, SF-V83, SF-V84) or seal failures, and high-level alarms to prevent cavity overflow onto the operating deck.
