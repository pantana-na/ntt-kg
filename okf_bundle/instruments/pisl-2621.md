# PISL-2621 — Spent Fuel Pool Skimmer Pump Suction Pressure Indicator & Switch Low

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> PISL-2621 protects Spent Fuel Pool Skimmer Pump P-12 against cavitation, dry-run damage, and loss of prime caused by low pool water level, clogged surface skimmer baskets (S-2A through S-2E), or suction line valve misalignment. Low suction pressure triggers a control room alarm and trips/interlocks pump P-12.

## 2. Process & Functional Architecture

- **Suction Line Monitoring:** PISL-2621 is connected off the 4" skimmer pump suction header through root valve `1-SF-V21`. This header draws suction from the five Spent Fuel Pool surface skimmers `S-2A` through `S-2E` as well as the alternate pool direct suction line via valve `1-SF-V17`.
- **Pump Protection Logic:** If Spent Fuel Pool surface skimmers experience flow blockage or the pool water level drops below skimmer weir elevations, PISL-2621 senses the vacuum drop / pressure reduction and de-energizes pump `P-12`, preventing seal degradation and pump runout.
