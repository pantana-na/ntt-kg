# FT-917 — High Head Safety Injection / Charging Main Header Flow Transmitter

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> FT-917 provides the primary Main Control Room indication of total high head emergency core cooling flow delivered to the reactor coolant system via containment penetration X-24 during Safety Injection actuation. Deviation between FT-917 and individual cold leg flow elements FE-924 through FE-927 indicates severe line breach or valve misposition inside containment.

## 2. Process & Functional Architecture

- **Emergency Core Cooling Monitoring:** In the event of a Loss of Coolant Accident (LOCA) or secondary break, both charging pumps deliver high pressure borated water into the common 4" HHI line. FT-917 measures total flow upstream of dual containment isolation valves `SI-V139` and `SI-V138` and penetration `X-24`.
- **Preoperational & Periodic Testing:** Test connections with valves CS-V68, CS-V67, CS-V753, CS-V754, and downstream test flow element FE-7448 permit periodic verification and in-service testing of flow measurement accuracy.
