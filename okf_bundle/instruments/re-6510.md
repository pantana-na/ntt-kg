# Instrument Specification: RE-6510 — Steam Generator 1 Blowdown Sample Radiation Monitor

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> RE-6510 monitors gross gamma activity in the cooled, pressure-regulated blowdown sample stream from Steam Generator 1 inside Skid RM-SKD-83. It is the primary continuous in-line detector for detecting primary-to-secondary steam generator tube leaks (SGTR). Sample flow must be confirmed by flow switch FS-6510 and flow indicator FI-6510; loss of sample flow invalidates leak detection capability.

## Functional Description & Operating Interlocks
* **Gross Activity Detection:** In-line scintillation detector continuously measures gamma activity of S/G 1 blowdown water to detect fission product leakage across S/G U-tubes.
* **Flow Confirmation:** Flow switch FS-6510 provides control room annunciation if sample flow through the detector chamber falls below minimum required threshold.
* **Selector Valving:** Triple-selector valve lineup (`RV-6510-1`, `RV-6510-2`, `RV-6510-3`) allows detector bypass, normal monitoring, or demineralized water flushing to decontaminate the detector chamber.
