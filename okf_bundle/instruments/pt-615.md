# PT-615 — RHR Loop B Suction Header Pressure Transmitter

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Residual Heat Removal Pump RH-P-8B suction pressure monitoring is safety-critical during cold shutdown and refueling operations. Inadvertent throttling of suction isolation valve RH-V40 or suction header voiding induces pump cavitation and loss of shutdown decay heat removal.

## Operating Philosophy & Functional Logic

* **Process Function:** Continuous electronic monitoring of static suction pressure in the 8" RHR Loop B suction line drawing from RCS Loop 4 Hot Leg (via penetration X-10) or Containment Sump B.
* **Redundant Train Architecture:** Completely independent from Train A instrument PT-614, providing physical and electrical separation across safety divisions.
* **Control Room Interface:** Transmits a continuous analog 4-20 mA pressure signal to Main Control Board instrumentation and plant process computers.
