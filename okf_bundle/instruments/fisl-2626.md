# FISL-2626 — Spent Fuel Pool Cleanup Return Header Flow Indicating Switch Low

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Minimum cleanup recirculation flow is required through Prefilter F-33, Demineralizer DM-8, and Postfilter F-34 to maintain spent fuel pool water clarity and boron concentration uniformity. A low-flow condition signals filter blinding, pump trip, or inadvertent valve misalignment, triggering a low-flow alarm to alert operators prior to pool thermal stratification.

## Operating Philosophy & Functional Logic

* **Process Function:** Monitors cleanup recirculation flow downstream of Fuel Pool Postfilter F-34 and Demineralizer DM-8 returning to the Spent Fuel Pool.
* **Low Flow Alarm Logic:** Upon flow dropping below the low setpoint, FISL-2626 actuates an annunciator in the Main Control Room, prompting operations to check Skimmer Pump P-12 discharge pressure (PI-2621) and filter differential pressures (PDIS-2622, PDIS-2623, PDIS-2624).
* **Interlocks:** Alerts operators to loss of cleanup flow; coordinates with skimmer pump trip circuitry.
