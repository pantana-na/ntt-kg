# LE-2385-1 / LE-2385-2 — Containment Recirculation Sump B Water Level Elements

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Containment Recirculation Sump B water level instrumentation provides safety-critical indication of flood inventory available for ECCS recirculation Train B (RHR Pump RH-P-8B and Containment Spray Pump CBS-P-9B). Interlock A (per Note 6) prevents opening Sump B isolation valve CBS-V14 until adequate flood level is established inside containment, ensuring pumps do not experience severe cavitation or vortex damage.

## Operating Philosophy & Functional Logic

* **Process Function:** Redundant safety-grade electronic level transmitters continuously measuring containment water inventory in Sump B following a design basis accident.
* **Train B Independence:** Operates completely independently from Sump A instrumentation (LE-2384-1/2), ensuring single-failure tolerance across ECCS recirculation trains.
* **Interlock A Logic:** Provides interlock permissive logic preventing opening of sump isolation MOV CBS-V14 until sufficient sump water depth guarantees adequate NPSH for pump RH-P-8B.
