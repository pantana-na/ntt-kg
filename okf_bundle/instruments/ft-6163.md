# Instrument Specification: FT-6163 — Cooling Tower Pump P-110A Discharge Flow Transmitter

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> FT-6163 monitors the 24" discharge flow rate of Ultimate Heat Sink (UHS) Cooling Tower Service Water Pump P-110A (Train A). Accurate flow transmission is essential to verify adequate decay heat removal capability and cooling tower recirculation flow during emergency ocean tunnel isolation or design basis accidents.

## Functional Description & Safety Interlocks
* **Flow Measurement:** Senses differential pressure across primary flow element FE-6163 installed downstream of pump P-110A discharge isolation valve V54.
* **Control Room Indication:** Transmits safety-related 4–20 mA flow signal to the Main Control Board for Train A cooling tower flow monitoring.
* **Technical Specification Verification:** Confirms pump performance against ASME Section XI in-service testing requirements and ultimate heat sink design basis flow requirements.
