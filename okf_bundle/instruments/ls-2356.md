# LS-2356 — Protective Chamber A Leak Detection Level Switch

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Level switch LS-2356 provides primary leak detection for the containment isolation boundary extension enclosing the 16" Containment Recirculation Sump A suction line, expansion bellows CBS-EP-36A, and motor-operated isolation valve CBS-V8. Activation of this switch in the Main Control Room signals a compromise of the containment boundary or active ECCS suction header during post-LOCA recirculation mode.

## 2. Process & Safety Functional Role

- **Leak Detection Monitoring:** LS-2356 is mounted to the lower shell of Protective Chamber CBS-TK-101A. The chamber is normally dry, sealed, and drained via locked-closed valves CBS-V92 and CBS-V127.
- **Accident Recirculation Protection:** During post-LOCA recirculation, borated coolant from Containment Recirculation Sump A is drawn through guard pipe 1-CBS-S-611-A, isolation valve CBS-V8, and expansion bellows CBS-EP-36A to supply RHR Pump RH-P-8A and Containment Spray Pump CBS-P-9A. Any packing or body gasket leakage from CBS-V8 or bellows fatigue failure is contained within CBS-TK-101A and immediately detected by LS-2356.
