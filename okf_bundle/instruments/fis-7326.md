# FIS-7326 — Charging Pump CS-P-2B Miniflow Interlock Flow Indicating Switch

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> FIS-7326 provides automatic low-flow protection for High Head Safety Injection / Charging Pump CS-P-2B. If forward discharge flow falls below minimum continuous flow limits, FIS-7326 commands motor-operated recirculation valve CS-V197 to open, routing fluid through bypass orifice FO-7482 back to the CVCS seal water return header to prevent pump overheating and deadheading damage.

## 2. Process & Safety Functional Role

- **Pump Miniflow Recirculation Logic:** Charging pump CS-P-2B requires continuous minimum flow to prevent casing thermal damage during closed-discharge or throttled operation. When total discharge flow measured by FE-7326 drops below setpoint, FIS-7326 commands valve `CS-V197` to open.
- **High Head Injection Alignment:** During safety injection actuation with substantial forward delivery, FIS-7326 directs `CS-V197` to close, concentrating pump throughput into the reactor coolant loops.
