# LE-2384-1 / LE-2384-2 — Containment Recirculation Sump A Water Level Elements

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Containment Recirculation Sump water level instrumentation is essential for post-LOCA emergency core cooling system (ECCS) operation. Interlock A (per Note 6) prevents opening Sump A isolation valve CBS-V8 until adequate flood level is established, preventing ECCS and containment spray pumps (RH-P-8A, CBS-P-9A) from vortexing or ingesting air during switchover from RWST injection to containment sump recirculation. Note that legacy level element LE-2350 is explicitly labeled as "NON-FUNCTIONAL" on drawing PID-1-SI-LR20448.

## Operating Philosophy & Functional Logic

* **Process Function:** Redundant safety-grade electronic level transmitters continuously monitoring water accumulation in Containment Recirculation Sump A following a primary coolant pipe break or steam line break inside containment.
* **ECCS Recirculation Permissive:** Signals that sufficient water has drained to the containment sump to provide Net Positive Suction Head (NPSH) for Residual Heat Removal Pump RH-P-8A and Containment Spray Pump CBS-P-9A.
* **Interlock A Logic:** Provides interlock permissive logic preventing opening of sump isolation MOV CBS-V8 until the predetermined post-LOCA water level threshold is satisfied, ensuring RWST injection mode is not terminated prematurely.
