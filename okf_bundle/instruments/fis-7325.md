# FIS-7325 — Charging Pump CS-P-2A Miniflow Interlock Flow Indicating Switch

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> FIS-7325 provides automatic low-flow protection for High Head Safety Injection / Charging Pump CS-P-2A. If forward discharge flow falls below minimum continuous flow limits, FIS-7325 commands motor-operated recirculation valve CS-V196 to open, routing fluid through bypass orifice FO-7466 back to the CVCS seal water return header to prevent pump overheating and deadheading damage.

## 2. Process & Safety Functional Role

- **Pump Miniflow Recirculation Logic:** High head multi-stage centrifugal charging pumps require continuous minimum flow to dissipate hydraulic heat and prevent casing thermal distortion or hydraulic instability. When total discharge flow measured by FE-7325 drops below setpoint, FIS-7325 commands valve `CS-V196` to open.
- **High Head Injection Alignment:** During large break LOCA or high safety injection demand where forward flow exceeds the miniflow setpoint, FIS-7325 signals `CS-V196` to close, ensuring 100% of pump output is injected into the RCS Cold Legs.
