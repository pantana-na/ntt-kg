# PDIS-2624 — Fuel Pool Demineralizer Differential Pressure Indicator & Switch

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> PDIS-2624 monitors differential pressure across the ion-exchange resin bed of Fuel Pool Demineralizer DM-8. Excessive bed differential pressure indicates resin compaction, bead fragmentation, or severe particulate clogging, which risks channel formation, poor deboronation/clean-up efficiency, or physical damage to resin retention underdrain strainers.

## 2. Process & Functional Architecture

- **Resin Bed Monitoring:** Demineralizer DM-8 provides ionic purification and radionuclide removal from spent fuel pool water. PDIS-2624 measures the pressure loss across the vessel internal distribution header, resin bed volume, and underdrain retention screens.
- **Operational Alignment:** When high $\Delta P$ is signaled, bypass valve `1-SF-V76` (4") can be opened to bypass the vessel, or spent resin sluicing operations can be initiated via sluice valves `1-SF-V49` and `1-SF-V57` to transfer spent resin to `RS-TK-79A & B`.
