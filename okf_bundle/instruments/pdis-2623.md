# PDIS-2623 — Fuel Pool Postfilter Differential Pressure Indicator & Switch

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> PDIS-2623 measures differential pressure across Fuel Pool Postfilter F-34. A sudden rise in differential pressure across F-34 indicates migration of resin fines from upstream Demineralizer DM-8 (due to resin underdrain strainer damage), while high steady DP indicates normal filter element loading that requires cartridge changeout before flow restrictions affect spent fuel pool cooling return.

## 2. Process & Functional Architecture

- **Resin Fine Trap Monitoring:** Postfilter F-34 is designed as a resin-trap filter downstream of Demineralizer DM-8 to prevent bead fragments from entering the Spent Fuel Pool. PDIS-2623 alerts operations to resin strainer failure or element loading.
- **Bypass Safeguard:** If differential pressure exceeds operating limits during critical pool cooling periods, bypass valve `1-SF-V37` (3") can be aligned to prevent flow starvation in the pool return header.
