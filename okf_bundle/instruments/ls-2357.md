# LS-2357 — Protective Chamber B Leak Detection Level Switch

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Level switch LS-2357 provides primary leak detection for the containment isolation boundary extension enclosing the 16" Containment Recirculation Sump B suction line, expansion bellows CBS-EP-36B, and motor-operated isolation valve CBS-V14. Activation of this switch in the Main Control Room signals a compromise of the containment boundary or active ECCS suction header during post-LOCA recirculation mode.

## 2. Process & Safety Functional Role

- **Leak Detection Monitoring:** LS-2357 is mounted to the lower shell of Protective Chamber CBS-TK-101B. The chamber is normally dry, sealed, and drained via locked-closed valves CBS-V92 and CBS-V128.
- **Accident Recirculation Protection:** During post-LOCA recirculation, borated coolant from Containment Recirculation Sump B is drawn through guard pipe 1-CBS-S-611-B, isolation valve CBS-V14, and expansion bellows CBS-EP-36B to supply RHR Pump RH-P-8B and Containment Spray Pump CBS-P-9B. Any packing or body gasket leakage from CBS-V14 or bellows fatigue failure is contained within CBS-TK-101B and immediately detected by LS-2357.
