# PDI-2682 — Reactor Cavity Cleanup Filter Differential Pressure Indicator

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> PDI-2682 provides local differential pressure indication across Reactor Cavity Cleanup Filter F-207 inside containment. High differential pressure indicates accumulation of particulate and crud from refueling pool drainage, necessitating filter cartridge replacement prior to cavitation of Refueling Canal Skimmer Pump P-272.

## 2. Process & Functional Architecture

- **Cavity Cleanup Filtration Monitoring:** Filter F-207 processes drainage from the Refueling Pool & Canal floor drains during refueling outages. PDI-2682 allows operators to track filter loading during post-refueling cavity drain-down and water clarity maintenance.
- **Pump Protection:** Filter F-207 is positioned directly on the suction side of Refueling Canal Skimmer Pump P-272. Maintaining clean filter elements prevents suction throttling and pump cavitation.
