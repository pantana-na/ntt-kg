# FT-922 — Intermediate Head Safety Injection Pump B Discharge Flow Transmitter

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> Intermediate Head Safety Injection Pump SI-P-6B provides redundant high-pressure safety injection to reactor coolant system hot legs and cold legs via penetrations X-26 and X-27. Accurate discharge flow indication from FT-922 is essential for ECCS performance evaluation and to verify that pump runout limits are maintained per Note 7 via throttle valve SI-V71.

## Operating Philosophy & Functional Logic

* **Process Function:** Monitors differential pressure across flow orifice FE-922 on the discharge of Safety Injection Pump SI-P-6B and transmits an analog signal to Main Control Board flow indicators and emergency response computers.
* **Redundancy & Independence:** Train B redundant counterpart to FT-918, powered from independent safety-related instrumentation power channels.
* **Injection Header Routing:** Senses intermediate-head injection flow split between Penetration X-26 (Hot Legs 2 and 3) and Penetration X-27 (Cold Legs A, B, C, and D).
