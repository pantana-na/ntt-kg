# PDIS-2622 — Fuel Pool Prefilter Differential Pressure Indicator & Switch

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> PDIS-2622 continuously measures differential pressure across Fuel Pool Prefilter F-33. High differential pressure indicates excessive particulate loading and element blinding, which can cause filter cartridge collapse, bypass of radioactive particulates into Demineralizer DM-8 resin bed, or upstream pump overpressure.

## 2. Process & Functional Architecture

- **Filter Integrity Monitoring:** Prefilter F-33 receives raw cleanup fluid from Skimmer Pump P-12, Spent Fuel Pool Cooling discharge, and CVCS purification headers. PDIS-2622 alerts operations when filter cartridge replacement or backwash/bypass is required before high differential pressure induces element degradation.
- **Bypass Safeguard:** Upon reaching high $\Delta P$ alarm setpoint, operators can align bypass valve `1-SF-V25` (4") around `F-33` to maintain continuous cleanup flow through downstream demineralization.
