# Instrument Specification: PDIS-8258 — Service Water Strainer S-10 Differential Pressure Indicating Switch

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> PDIS-8258 monitors differential pressure across nuclear safety-related Service Water Strainer S-10 (Train B). Fouling or marine debris accumulation can starve cooling water to Primary Component Cooling Water Heat Exchanger CC-E-17B and Emergency Diesel Generator Jacket Cooler DG-E-42A. Inadvertent opening of bypass valve V70 to relieve high DP introduces unfiltered raw ocean water into nuclear safety cooling loads.

## Functional Description & Safety Interlocks
* **Differential Pressure Indication:** Provides local visual indication of pressure drop across the internal strainer basket of S-10.
* **High Differential Pressure Alarm:** Actuates Main Control Board high DP annunciation when debris accumulation restricts seawater flow through the strainer basket.
* **Automatic Backwash Initiation:** Triggers automatic backwash cycle / backwash arm rotation and flush discharge to restore low differential pressure across S-10 without interrupting continuous cooling flow.
